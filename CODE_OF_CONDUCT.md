# Community Code of Conduct

NovelForge AI welcomes constructive participation from people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's credentials, writing, local
  data, or identifying information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and author control.
- Give credit for work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, doxxing, credential exposure, and sustained disruptive conduct
are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical.
